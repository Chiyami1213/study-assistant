**How do I get my homework and exams into the Task List?** Open Calendar > Task List > **Import From Syllabus**. Tick the courses (the active courses of the term are ticked; a self-study course is
optional), and the app reads what is already in each course page and in its syllabus file in the Library, and adds every new task. Change or delete any task afterwards; one course can be checked row by row first.

**How do I change the term, the dates or the wake-up time?** The term list above the calendar switches the term; its last entry, Manage Terms, changes the dates and has Add New Term (with its wake-up
time, bedtime and holidays).

**How do I bring in my Canvas, Brightspace or Google Calendar?** Calendar > Task List > **Import From Calendar**. Copy the private calendar link of your service (Canvas: Calendar > Calendar Feed;
Brightspace: Calendar > Subscribe; Google Calendar: Settings > the calendar > Integrate calendar > Secret address in iCal format), paste it, read it, check the table and add. The link is kept on this
computer and read again to keep your tasks and events up to date (**Sync Now**, or when the app opens). Without a link, import an `.ics` file (Google Calendar: Settings > Import & export > Export).

**How do I see the getting-started tutorial again?** Click **Show the Tutorial** under the title of the Home page (it is there once you skipped or finished it), or use Settings > Help > **Show the Tutorial Again**.

**How do I get study guides, helpers and grade pages made by an AI assistant into the app?** Library > Import > **Prompt for AI**: choose the course, the kind of page and its number, and your assistant
(**Claude**, **ChatGPT** or **Gemini**), copy the prompt into a chat with it and attach your course files (the prompt lists which). The assistant writes one HTML page in the format the app reads; download it, keep the file name the prompt gave, and import it with
**Add a File** (or a zip of several). The app recognizes the type from the name (Notes, Homework Helper, Grade Explanation, Exam Reflection, Exam Preparation ...), pairs a helper or a grade
explanation with its homework or exam, and opens the links between your pages.

**How do I add many files at once, or keep the Library up to date?** Library > **Import** has **Import a Zip** (a zip of pdf and html files) and **Update From Folder** (the folder your files are kept
in; also the **Update Files** button on a course page). Both show what they found, with the type, title and course of each, and import only after you confirm. The folder is only read: your files there
are never changed or deleted.

**How do I install or update it?** See [Install](INSTALL.md). The Windows setup wizard detects an installed version and offers to update it, to uninstall it
first, or to keep both. On a Mac, drag the app over the old one and choose Replace.

**Windows or my antivirus warns about the app.** It is a false alarm (see the note under Download and install): choose *More info > Run anyway*, or allow the file. On a Mac,
right-click the app and choose *Open*.

**Where are my files?** `%APPDATA%\Study Assistant` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/Study Assistant` on a Mac. Updating or reinstalling never touches them.

**How do I back up, or move to another computer?** Settings > Backup keeps a copy of your Library; *Export My Files* makes one zip, and *Import* on the other computer brings it
back (a Windows export can be imported on a Mac and the other way round).

**The window says "Connection error" or stops responding.** Close it and open Study Assistant again; your files are safe. The installed app keeps a log in the `logs` folder of the
data folder: include its last lines when you report the problem.

**The function keys do nothing on my Mac laptop.** Hold the **fn** key with F2, F5 and F11, or turn on "Use F1, F2, etc. keys as standard function keys" in System Settings.
Screenshots (F2) ask for the Screen Recording permission the first time.

**How do I uninstall it?** Windows: Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Study Assistant > Uninstall (it asks whether to keep your files). Mac: drag the app from Applications to the Bin.

**Something is not working, or I have an idea.** Open **Settings > Report an Issue** in the app: it opens a ready-made report on GitHub (you read it and press Submit; the app sends
nothing by itself). Or [open an issue](https://github.com/Chiyami1213/study-assistant/issues/new/choose) here. A free GitHub account is needed, and issues are public, so leave out anything personal.
